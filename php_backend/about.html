About SmartPresence

Integrated platform for face recognition attendance, automated billing & subscription management

Project Overview

SmartPresence is an innovative solution designed to revolutionize workforce management by combining facial recognition attendance with automated wage calculation, statutory deductions (EPF, ESIC, PT, Bonus) and organization subscription billing. Developed by Sakib Shiakh, this platform streamlines HR operations, eliminates manual errors, and ensures compliance with labor laws.

Using OpenCV for real-time face detection and PHP/MySQL for backend processing, the system captures attendance, categorizes employees by skill (Unskilled, Semi-skilled, Skilled, Highly Skilled), and generates accurate wage musters with applicable deductions and contributions. Subscription plans allow organizations to choose features based on size and needs.

SmartPresence is ideal for factories, construction sites, corporate offices, and any organization requiring precise attendance-linked payroll processing.

Core Objectives

Automated Attendance

Face recognition-based marking with anti-spoofing to prevent proxy.

Wage Calculation

Daily wages based on skill category (Unskilled ₹805, Semi ₹893, Skilled ₹981, High ₹1065).

Statutory Deductions

Auto‑calculates EPF (12%), ESIC (0.75% for unskilled), Professional Tax (slab‑based), Bonus (8.33% for unskilled).

Wage Muster Generation

Generates Excel/PDF wage registers with all earnings and deductions per employee.

Subscription Plans

Organizations can choose from Basic, Pro, or Enterprise plans based on employee count and features.

Admin Dashboard

Real‑time overview of attendance, category‑wise employee count, pending approvals, and contact queries.

How It Works

1

Organization Registers

Company signs up, selects a subscription plan, and uploads registration certificate.

2

Employee Enrollment

HR adds employees with name, photo, Aadhar, mobile, DOB, and skill category.

3

Daily Attendance

Employees mark presence via face recognition camera; system records time‑in and status.

4

Wage & Billing

Attendance data is used to compute daily wages, deductions, and generate wage muster reports.

5

Admin Oversight

Organization admin views dashboards, employee lists, attendance history, and contact messages.
